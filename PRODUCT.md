# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Knowledge workers, students, engineers, and creators who need structured focus sessions (Pomodoro technique) while monitoring and mitigating mental fatigue and stress levels, complemented by background focus audio.

## Product Purpose

CortiTick provides a holistic focus workstation that pairs a Pomodoro timer and task list with a dynamic Cortisol (stress) indicator and YouTube focus sound player. It makes burnout risks visible and gives users immediate musical and behavioral tools to sustain mental clarity.

## Positioning

Unlike generic countdown timers that treat work time as purely mechanical, CortiTick models physiological strain (cortisol accumulation and decompression) and integrates custom audio study queues directly into the productivity flow.

## Operating Context

- Used in desktop and laptop web browsers during extended study or work blocks.
- Frequently kept open in a split window or secondary monitor.
- Accompanied by headphones or desk speakers playing focus/lo-fi/ambient audio.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Pomodoro Timer**: Configurable Work (1–120 min) and Break (1–60 min) intervals, auto-transitioning, clean circular time display, start/pause/reset.
- **Cortisol Gauge**: Reactive semicircular gauge translating work duration, break compliance, and task load into real-time stress feedback (0–100%, Low/Normal/High).
- **Task Management**: Real-time task creation, completion toggle, deletion, and segregation between pending and completed items.
- **Focus Music & Saved Library**: YouTube playback for single videos, official playlists, and user-curated Saved Library queues with auto-advance, loop, and shuffle.
- **Preferences & Themes**: Seamless Dark and Light appearance modes, persistent library and audio volume across sessions.
- **Static Export**: Must compile to a static client-side bundle for GitHub Pages hosting (no backend server dependencies).

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone**: Focused, calm, clinical yet warm, supportive, scientific without being cold.
- **Colors**: Deep midnight slate base with thoughtful violet/indigo structure, energetic emerald break accents, and alert amber/red warning states.
- **Typography**: Legible, high-contrast, robust tabular numbers for timers and percentages.

## Product Principles

1. **Visibility without Distraction**: Data and audio should support focus, not become a game or source of anxiety.
2. **Seamless Audio Continuity**: Music in the library must flow smoothly between tracks without requiring manual tab switching or repetitive inputs.
3. **Responsive Dignity**: Works cleanly from a compact 375px mobile viewport to an expansive 4K ultra-wide monitor.
4. **Resilient Local-First**: All settings, tasks, and music queues survive page refreshes with zero server dependencies.
